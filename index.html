About

I'm a third-year Electrical Engineering student at the University of Toronto, passionate about power systems and control systems that shape how energy is generated, distributed, and consumed.

I'm pursuing a minor in Sustainable Energy to build a foundation in energy production, storage, distribution, and policy. My coursework and projects span analog circuit design, power electronics, digital systems, and embedded development — giving me a hands-on understanding of both the hardware and the systems-level thinking that engineering demands.

Beyond the lab, I build software projects ranging from FPGA-based games to web apps. I enjoy working at the intersection of hardware and software, where clean design meets real-world constraints.
